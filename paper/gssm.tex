\begin{abstract}
We show that the geometric selective state-space model (GSSM-Selective) is the general
input-controlled reproducing-kernel operator of the linear state-space family: Mamba/S6,
S5, and the Linear Recurrent Unit (LRU) are switch-restrictions of a single associative
affine prefix scan, verified against the sequential recurrence to $\sim\!10^{-15}$ in
double precision for every family member. Freezing the gates to constants collapses the
selective operator exactly onto the stationary geometric Toeplitz kernel
$\gamma^{|s-t|}$: a BPTT-trained constant-gate model reproduces the closed-form kernel to
$3.55\times10^{-15}$ at width $d=512$, while a genuinely selective control departs from any
single geometric kernel by $4.9\times10^{-2}$ --- a control/match ratio of $1.37\times10^{13}$
that certifies the identity is structural, not coincidental. Three consequences follow from
one operator. First, the scan carries no absolute-position term: its memory kernel depends
only on relative lags, so the model is length-invariant \emph{by construction}. Removing the
sinusoidal positional encoding collapses length drift from $+243\%$ to $+2.6\%$ (train
$T{=}32$, evaluated frozen out to $T{=}1024$), and across five seeds the position-free variant
holds $\times0.93\pm0.00$ at $256\times$ its training length while the encoded variant breaks to
$\times7.05\pm2.34$; the same weights stream a billion tokens at a flat $4.36$~GB. Second, the
per-channel readout is a rank-1 functional of the feature history, which we turn into a
capacity theorem: exact associative recall demands rank-$K$ binding, so a bounded scalar
channel is provably capped near chance --- measured at $14\%$ multi-query recall against a
$1.56\%$ floor --- while one attention layer restores full recall, but \emph{only} when the
recurrent scan is selective. A double dissociation makes this causal: two architectures
identical to the layer (three SSM layers plus one attention layer) that differ \emph{only} in
scan class reach $100\%$ versus $16\%$ recall, because the selective interior attractor keeps
the residual stream legible for attention to bind while the gate-free boundary attractor
saturates and corrupts it. The kernel view thus \emph{computes} the recall ceiling that the
standard reading merely observes, and shows that attractor topology --- a property of the
kernel's DC gain --- causally gates whether attention can bind. We position the result as a
subsumption of the linear-SSM family rather than a competitor within it.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

The last three years produced a family of subquadratic sequence models --- S4
\cite{gu2022s4}, S5 \cite{smith2023s5}, LRU \cite{orvieto2023lru}, Mamba/S6
\cite{gu2023mamba}, and the recurrent-linear-attention lineage RWKV \cite{peng2023rwkv},
DeltaNet \cite{yang2024deltanet}, xLSTM \cite{beck2024xlstm} --- each of which trades the
quadratic cost and $O(T)$ key--value cache of attention \cite{vaswani2017attention} for a
bounded recurrent state. These architectures are usually presented as distinct designs. We
argue they are not: they are parametric restrictions of one operator, and the shared
structure is what determines both what they can and what they structurally cannot do.

We study the geometric selective state-space model (GSSM-Selective), the engineering
instantiation of a body of work on M\"obius coupling and doubly-stochastic spectra
\cite{foss2026markov,foss2026ginibre,foss2026gssm}. Per channel it runs the gated leaky
recurrence
\begin{equation}
z_t = \gamma_t\,z_{t-1} + a_t,\qquad
a_t = \alpha_t\,\phi(\vbar_t),\qquad
\phi(v)=\log(1-v^2),\qquad
s_t = \sqrt{1-e^{z_t}}\in[0,1),
\label{eq:core}
\end{equation}
with $\vbar_t = g_t v_t$ the gated velocity, $v_t=\tanh(W_v x_t)$, and
$\gamma_t,\alpha_t,g_t$ input-dependent sigmoid gates. Our contribution is to read this one
recurrence three ways and show the readings are the same object.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Kernel unification (\S\ref{sec:kernel}).} GSSM-Selective is the general
affine reproducing-kernel operator of the linear-SSM family; Mamba/S6, S5, and LRU are
three-switch restrictions of it, verified to $\sim\!10^{-15}$ in \code{float64}. Its LTI
restriction is \emph{literally} the geometric Toeplitz kernel, matched to $3.55\times10^{-15}$
at $d{=}512$ with a $1.37\times10^{13}$ control/match separation.
\item \textbf{Length invariance (\S\ref{sec:length}).} The kernel has no absolute-position
term, so the scan needs no positional encoding and is length-invariant by construction. We
give the mechanism and the clean $2\times2$ ablation: drift $+243\%\to+2.6\%$ on removing
the PE, flat perplexity to $524{,}288\times$ training length, a billion tokens at constant
memory.
\item \textbf{The capacity ladder and the double dissociation (\S\ref{sec:capacity}).} The
per-channel readout is rank-1, which we convert into an associative-recall capacity theorem
that \emph{predicts} the measured scalar-recall wall. A double dissociation --- two
layer-identical stacks differing only in scan class, at $100\%$ vs.\ $16\%$ recall --- shows
that the attractor topology (a DC-gain property of the kernel) causally gates whether an
attention layer can bind.
\end{enumerate}
Every number in this paper is measured on an Apple M4 Mac mini (PyTorch 2.9.1, offline) and
is reproducible from a committed script and a result JSON; we cite the JSON at each claim.

\section{The one operator and its reproducing kernel}
\label{sec:kernel}

\subsection{Unrolling to an inner product}
Unrolling \eqref{eq:core} with $z_{-1}=0$ gives, exactly,
\begin{equation}
z_t \;=\; \sum_{k=0}^{t} \underbrace{\Big(\alpha_k\!\!\prod_{j=k+1}^{t}\gamma_j\Big)}_{w_t[k]}\,\phi(\vbar_k)
\;=\; \langle w_t,\ \phi(\vbar_{0:t})\rangle,
\qquad \Gk := \prod_{j=k+1}^{t}\gamma_j .
\label{eq:innerprod}
\end{equation}
The state is a single inner product of an input-controlled weight vector $w_t$ against the
feature history $\phi(\vbar_{0:t})$, and the readout $s_t=\phi^{-1}(z_t)=\sqrt{1-e^{z_t}}$ is
a fixed monotone bijection that adds no representational dimension. The feature map
$\phi(v)=\log(1-v^2)$ is the rapidity coordinate: writing $v=\tanh\xi$,
$\phi(\tanh\xi)=-2\log\cosh\xi$, so the scan composes drives additively in the boost
coordinate of $SO(1,1)$ --- the analytic backbone of the M\"obius/Minkowski framing
\cite{foss2026markov}. The inner-product form reproduces the executed (gated, clamped) code
recurrence to $8\times10^{-9}$, the idealized recurrence to $5.5\times10^{-17}$ in
\code{float64}, and the rapidity identity to $1\times10^{-14}$.

The induced per-path time--time Gram
\begin{equation}
K^{(x)}(s,t) \;=\; \sum_{k\le\min(s,t)} \alpha_k^2\,\Gamma_{k\to s}\,\Gamma_{k\to t}
\label{eq:kernel}
\end{equation}
is the reproducing object. It is positive semidefinite for \emph{every} input path --- it is
the Gram $WW^\top$ of explicit real weight vectors, so
$\sum_{s,t} c_s c_t K^{(x)}(s,t)=\|\sum_t c_t w_t\|^2\ge 0$ --- with measured minimum
eigenvalue $\ge -10^{-12}$ over 40{,}000 adversarial gate paths. \PROVEN

\paragraph{What is, and is not, claimed.} The kernel \eqref{eq:kernel} is a data-dependent,
time-inhomogeneous (causal Volterra) object, \emph{not} a fixed Mercer kernel: the weights
$\alpha_k(x)$, $\gamma_j(x)$ and the features $\phi(\vbar_k(x))$ all depend on the same $x$,
so there is no fixed $k(v,v')$ in raw velocity space, and ``replace BPTT with ridge
regression on a fixed Mercer kernel'' is \emph{false} for the selective model (two inputs
with identical $\phi_k$ but different gate context give different $z_T$, $-1.38$ vs.\ $-0.44$).
The reproducing-readout property is genuine and exact; the fixed-Mercer reading survives only
in the LTI limit below. Stating this boundary precisely is part of the contribution.

\subsection{The family as three switches}
Every model in the linear-SSM family is a first-order recurrence
$h_t=A_t h_{t-1}+B_t u_t$ carrying $(A_t,B_t)$ under the \emph{identical} associative affine
operator
\begin{equation}
(A_2,B_2)\otimes(A_1,B_1)=(A_2 A_1,\ A_2 B_1 + B_2),\qquad \text{identity }(1,0),
\label{eq:monoid}
\end{equation}
which a parallel prefix scan computes. The family members differ only in three parametric
switches: the state algebra of $A$, whether $A_t$ is input-dependent, and the drive map for
$B_t$ (Table~\ref{tab:family}). GSSM-Selective is their common generalization: the affine
prefix scan with a nonlinear rapidity feature and a bounded decoder $\phi^{-1}$ that
\emph{guarantees} $s_t\in[0,1)$ --- a boundedness guarantee Mamba lacks.

The dictionary is one equivalence: \emph{selective} $\Leftrightarrow$ $A_t$
\emph{input-dependent} $\Leftrightarrow$ the kernel is time-inhomogeneous, not Mercer. Mamba
is input-gated and hence \emph{also} not a fixed Mercer kernel, for exactly the reason GSSM
is not; S5 and LRU freeze $A$ to a time-constant and so collapse to a genuine fixed
complex-exponential (LTI) kernel. GSSM's constant-$\gamma$ limit $\gamma^{|s-t|}$ is the real,
scalar, one-pole sibling of that kernel.

\subsection{The LTI restriction is the geometric Toeplitz kernel}
\label{sec:toeplitz}
The sharpest form of the unification is a falsifiable identity. Freeze the gates to
time-constants ($\gamma_t\equiv\gamma$, $\alpha_t\equiv\alpha$) and the layer's temporal
operator becomes the geometric Toeplitz convolution $\gamma^{|s-t|}$ \emph{by construction}.
We train a GSSM-Selective with frozen $W_\gamma,W_\alpha$ by BPTT and compare its learned read
map against the closed-form kernel $z=K\cdot a$. The trained map \emph{is} the kernel, at
every width tested:

\subsection{The scan is $O(\log T)$-depth and exact to the loop}
Because \eqref{eq:monoid} is associative, the scan parallelizes by a Hillis--Steele doubling
prefix scan --- $O(\log T)$ depth instead of $O(T)$. We wired it into the reference layer's
forward \emph{and} backward with zero edits to the frozen module. Forward and gradient are
identical to the sequential loop: in \code{float64}, forward $\max|\Delta|=1.67\times10^{-16}$
and gradient (all parameters and the input) $\max|\Delta|=3.55\times10^{-15}$;
\code{gradcheck} passes for $T\in\{1,2,3,7,8,17,32,33\}$; training loss curves coincide to
$4.8\times10^{-7}$ over 12 steps. On MPS the doubling scan beats the loop $3.4$--$3.9\times$
across $T\in\{128,512,1024,2048\}$ (scan depth $7,9,10,11$); on CPU the tight loop wins, so
the dispatcher routes GPU/MPS to the doubling scan and CPU to the sequential loop. We claim
constant inference state with no KV-cache, and now a measured --- not aspirational ---
parallel-scan training speedup. (Source:
\code{results/parallel\_scan\_integration\_results.json}.) \PROVEN

\section{Length invariance: the kernel carries no absolute position}
\label{sec:length}

\subsection{Mechanism}
Read \eqref{eq:innerprod}: every dependence on the time index enters \emph{only} through the
relative products $\Gk=\prod_{j=k+1}^t\gamma_j$ --- through lags $t-k$, never through the
absolute coordinate $t$. There is no term $f(t)$ anywhere in the operator. A causal kernel
whose only index dependence is lag is shift-equivariant in time \emph{by construction}, so its
readout at step $t$ is identical in distribution whether $t=20$ or $t=8000$: position is not
in the kernel. \PROVEN{} In the constant-gate limit this is literally the stationary
(Toeplitz) exponential kernel $\gamma^{|s-t|}=e^{-|s-t|/\tau}$, $\tau=-1/\log\gamma$, which
Bochner certifies strictly positive-definite (its DTFT is the positive Poisson kernel; realized
Gram min eigenvalue $+0.32$). A sinusoidal positional encoding is the \emph{only} term that
references absolute $t$; beyond the training length it is out of distribution, and it is the
sole source of any break. The selective gate is separately necessary: it keeps the contraction
$\tau<1$ so the receptive field stays bounded ($\approx5$--$8$ tokens from the learned gates)
and does not grow with $T$; drop it (the ``Pure'' variant) and the DC gain $\alpha/(1-\gamma)$
diverges, driving $s_t\to 1$ at the boundary. Length invariance needs \emph{both}: no absolute
position \emph{and} the gate.

\subsection{The clean $2\times2$ ablation}
We train four arms at $T{=}32$ and evaluate with frozen weights out to $T{=}1024$
(Table~\ref{tab:length}, Figure~\ref{fig:length}). Selective-NoPE holds essentially flat while
Selective+PE, identical up to the positional encoding, degrades monotonically; the gate-free
Pure variant explodes and the Transformer drifts then, past its fixed PE buffer, cannot execute
at all. The drift reported is measured from $T{=}128$ to $T{=}1024$ (the regime past the
training window), directly from the result JSON.

\subsection{The invariance is a structural constant, not a lucky run}
The result is seed-invariant and extends far past $T{=}1024$.
\begin{itemize}
\sloppy
\item \textbf{Five seeds.} Over seeds $\{1,7,42,123,2024\}$ at $256\times$ ($T{=}8192$),
Selective-NoPE holds $\times0.93\pm0.00$ (the standard deviation rounds to zero --- every seed
lands on the same flat line), while Selective+PE breaks to $\times7.05\pm2.34$ on every seed.
(Source: \code{results/length\_seed\_robustness\_d128.json}.)
\item \textbf{The smoking gun.} NoPE's learned gate statistics are frozen across the $256\times$
span ($\gamma_\text{mean}: 0.2349\to0.2350$; $\gamma_{p95}: 0.4126\to0.4131$) --- the operator
is literally in-distribution at every length, because it has no length-coupled quantity to
drift. With the PE present, the gates visibly drift ($\gamma_\text{mean}\;0.237\to0.277$) to
compensate, and break.
\item \textbf{To the wall.} Using the $O(1)$ recurrent forward, the same $T{=}32$-trained model
holds $\times0.98$ at $T{=}131{,}072$ ($4096\times$), on both WikiText-2 and, re-run,
WikiText-103. (Source: \code{results/scale\_to\_the\_wall.json}, \code{results/scale\_wt103.json}.)
\item \textbf{Constant memory to a billion tokens.} Because the receptive field is
$\approx5$--$8$ tokens, chunked streaming with overlap $\gg$ that field reproduces the
whole-sequence computation exactly. A single $16.7$-million-token sequence evaluates at a
constant $2.5$~GB with perplexity \emph{improving} to $\times0.80$; a lazily streamed billion
tokens of C4 ($31{,}250{,}432\times$ training length) runs at a flat $4.36$~GB, the running
perplexity moving $1.3$ points across twenty $50$M-token checkpoints. (Source:
\code{results/scale\_to\_a\_million.json}, \code{results/scale\_to\_a\_billion.json}.)
\end{itemize}
This is the axis where a bounded state wins by construction rather than by parameters or data:
attention pays $O(T)$ cache, $O(T^2)$ compute, and a positional code that fails out of
distribution, and a fixed PE buffer ties it to a maximum length --- the same failure that
crashes both Selective+PE and the Transformer past their buffer, while NoPE has no such ceiling.

\section{The capacity ladder and the double dissociation}
\label{sec:capacity}

The same structural fact that makes the state bounded, scannable, and cache-free ---
$z_t=\langle w_t,\phi(\vbar_{0:t})\rangle$ is a \emph{scalar} inner product --- caps its
associative-recall capacity. We make this quantitative, then close it causally: a double
dissociation shows the scan class alone --- not the architecture --- decides whether an
attention layer can bind.

\subsection{A rank-1 readout cannot bind $K$ pairs}
\begin{proposition}[Rank-1 per channel]
Each channel's state \eqref{eq:innerprod} is a rank-1 functional of its feature history: the
entire history $\phi(\vbar_{0:t})$ is collapsed onto one real number, and the readout
$s_t=\phi^{-1}(z_t)$ is a fixed bijection adding no dimension. \PROVEN
\end{proposition}

Exact multi-query associative recall (MQAR \cite{arora2024zoology}) over $K$ distinct keys
requires recovering an association matrix $\hat A$ with $\arg\max_v \hat A[k_i,v]=v_i$ for all
$i$; the sufficient memory is the outer-product $A=\sum_i e_{k_i}e_{v_i}^\top$ of rank $K$.
Attention realizes $A$ directly and hence recalls exactly. A bank of $D$ scalar channels read
by a fixed linear map spans association matrices of dimension $\le D$, so the number of exactly
recoverable pairs is bounded by the readout rank (the rest are correct only at chance $1/V$);
the optimal achievable recall is the Eckart--Young rank-$D$ truncation of $A$. \PROVEN

\begin{theorem}[Scalar-SSM associative-recall ceiling]
\label{thm:rank}
For a layer of $D$ scalar leaky-integrator channels read by a fixed input-independent map, on
MQAR over $K$ keys with values in $[V]$: (i) the recovered association has rank $\le D$, with
best-case recall on the Eckart--Young curve; (ii) attention attains rank $K$ and recall $1$;
(iii) the \emph{binding} rank attainable by the leaky-scalar mechanism itself is $1$ per
channel, because its increment $a_t=\alpha_t\phi(\vbar_t)$ is a function of the current token
only, with no key-conditioned (outer-product) write --- so the effective binding rank of the
trained scalar stack is $O(1)$, far below $D$. \ARGUED{} for (iii); \PROVEN{} for (i)--(ii).
\end{theorem}

The decisive empirical signature is that pure-scalar recall is \emph{flat in the query gap}, not
a decay cliff: a memory-decay limit would fall off with distance; a capacity limit is flat, and
the measured profile is a constant floor at every gap. The data say capacity, not forgetting.
With $K{=}8$, $V{=}64$ and effective binding rank $D_\text{eff}{\approx}1$, the closed-form
prediction is $D_\text{eff}/K+(1-D_\text{eff}/K)/V\approx0.139$, and the Eckart--Young best case
is $0.186$ --- a bracket the measured scalar recall of $\approx0.14$ (train len 64) lands
inside; inverting the bound gives $D_\text{eff}\approx1.02$. The framework computes its own
floor rather than excusing it.

\subsection{The wall, measured; and a cache-free lift}
The standalone selective scalar tops out at $14.1\%$ (train len 64) / $14.5\%$ (test len 256)
on MQAR --- the wall the theorem predicts at $D_\text{eff}{\approx}1$, and the sel4 (SSSS) arm
of the dissociation below (Source: \code{evidence\_companion/hybrid\_B.json}). A separate,

lifted \emph{without} an attention layer. The lever is a \emph{key-conditioned} write: carry a
complex leaky accumulator $S_t=\gamma_t S_{t-1}+u_t e^{i\varphi_t}$ with key angle
$\varphi_t=\pi\tanh(W_\text{key}x_t)$ (token identity, not time), read at a query by de-rotation
$\mathrm{Re}(S_t e^{-i\varphi_q})$ --- the complex analogue of attention's outer-product binding,
in $O(1)$ state with no KV-cache. This clears both chance and the $3.72$~pp noise band
(Table~\ref{tab:recall}).

coordinate, and the framework draws the whole ladder: scalar (rank 1, recall $O(1/K)$) $\to$
complex/phase (rank 2) $\to$ bounded fast-weight (rank $D$, cf.\ DeltaNet \cite{yang2024deltanet})
$\to$ attention (rank $K$, exact). The wall is the bottom rung of a ladder the operator algebra
predicts, not a hole in it.

\subsection{The double dissociation: scan class alone gates binding}
\label{sec:dissociation}
The sharpest form of the capacity claim is causal, and it is the reason the family's hybrids
work. Take two architectures identical down to the layer --- three SSM layers plus one attention
layer --- and vary \emph{only} the scan class of the SSM layers: SSAS uses the selective scan,
PPAP the gate-free (``Pure'') scan. Everything else, including the attention layer, is held fixed.
The outcome flips completely (Table~\ref{tab:dissociation}, Figure~\ref{fig:dissociation}).

On MQAR (train len 64, eval len 256), SSAS reaches $100.0\%$ recall and PPAP reaches $16.1\%$ ---
the same $16\%$ floor as the standalone selective scalar (sel4, $14.5\%$) and its Pure sibling,
because a single attention layer atop a \emph{saturated} residual stream cannot bind. The
attention-only control (AAAA) also reaches $100\%$, and putting the attention layer at the top
instead of the middle (SSSA, $99.9\%$) leaves the result intact: it is the scan class, not the
attention placement, that decides. On the length task (train $T{=}32$, eval $T{=}256$), the same
split appears in extrapolation: SSAS drifts $+35.0\%$ and the all-selective sel4 $+38.8\%$, while
PPAP explodes $+313.4\%$ --- the boundary attractor that corrupts recall also breaks length.

The mechanism is the attractor topology of \S\ref{sec:length}. The selective interior attractor
($s^\ast\!<\!1$, finite DC gain $\alpha/(1-\gamma)$) keeps the residual stream legible, so the
attention layer can read the keys it needs to bind; the Pure boundary attractor ($s^\ast\!\to\!1$,
DC gain $\to\infty$) saturates and corrupts the residual, so the identical attention layer has
nothing clean to bind. This is the causal complement to Theorem~\ref{thm:rank}: the theorem says a
scalar channel \emph{cannot} bind $K$ pairs alone; the dissociation says one attention layer
\emph{restores} full binding, but only when the recurrent scan's attractor is interior. Attractor
topology --- a DC-gain property of the kernel --- causally gates whether attention works.

\section{Secondary results and the honest limits}
\label{sec:secondary}

\paragraph{Width stabilization (optimization, not architecture).} A width-$d512$ collapse in the
base architecture is an optimization-at-width artifact, not a capacity ceiling. The root cause is
an unbounded value projection running hot at width: at $d512$ the value-projection preactivation
peaks at $\|W_v\|_\text{max}{=}239$ and velocity saturation is $0.434$, and the model sits at
$202.0$ perplexity. $\mu$P alone drops velocity saturation to $0.049$ ($\sim\!9\times$) and the
preactivation to $20.6$; the full stabilization stack ($\mu$P $+$ preLN $+$ warmup) reaches
$152.9$ perplexity, matching the $d256$ level, and the Pure variant recovers too ($310\to191$)
(Figure~\ref{fig:widthfix}, Figure~\ref{fig:attribution}; Source:
\code{evidence\_companion/width\_fix.json}). The architecture then scales without collapse across
$d256$--$d1024\times L2$--$L4$, best $135.2$ at $d512/L4$, with $d1024/L4$ skipped for RAM rather
than collapse (Figure~\ref{fig:scaleup}; Source: \code{evidence\_companion/scaleup.json}).

\paragraph{Limits, computed rather than excused.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{The scalar recall wall is real.} A bounded scalar state cannot do exact
associative recall alone (Theorem~\ref{thm:rank}); $14\%$ standalone ($1.7\%$ on the stricter
O1 harness) is the operator-class limit, not a training artifact. One attention layer restores
full recall in the selective hybrid (SSAS $100\%$, the double dissociation), and the
cache-free holographic write lifts the standalone scalar to $8.9\%$ --- genuine but modest.
The standalone scalar limit is structural.
\item \textbf{No power-law claim.} The perplexity floor of $\approx135$ across the scale grid is
the WikiText-2 data budget ($\sim\!1.7$M tokens), not the architecture; more parameters do not
move it, more data would. We read the flat plateau through the fixed-rank-per-channel kernel
lens, and label that reading an \ANALOGY{}, not a derived bound.
\item \textbf{No group-structured state tracking (TC$^0$).} A bounded scalar state cannot
represent the requisite group structure; this is derived, not yet empirically stressed.
\end{itemize}

\section{Related work and positioning}

The linear-SSM literature --- S4 \cite{gu2022s4}, S5 \cite{smith2023s5}, LRU
\cite{orvieto2023lru}, Mamba/S6 \cite{gu2023mamba} --- and the recurrent-linear-attention
lineage RWKV \cite{peng2023rwkv}, DeltaNet \cite{yang2024deltanet}, xLSTM \cite{beck2024xlstm}
each present a distinct architecture. Our contribution is orthogonal: we show these are
switch-restrictions of one affine prefix-scan operator (Table~\ref{tab:family}), so the family's
shared behavior --- length handling, recall capacity, boundedness --- is a property of the common
operator, read off its kernel. S5 and LRU are the LTI (fixed-Mercer) corner; Mamba and GSSM are
the selective (time-inhomogeneous) interior; the delta-rule and fast-weight models
\cite{yang2024deltanet} are the rank-$D$ rung of the capacity ladder that
Theorem~\ref{thm:rank} organizes.

The affine prefix-scan formulation of linear recurrences is itself classical
\cite{blelloch1990prefix} and was applied to linear RNNs by Martin and Cundy
\cite{martin2018parallelizing}; S5 \cite{smith2023s5} runs on an associative scan
explicitly. What is new here is not the scan but the reading: the three-switch taxonomy
verified member-by-member at machine precision, the reproducing-kernel structure with its
precise Mercer/non-Mercer boundary, and the consequences (length invariance, capacity,
attractor topology) derived from that one object. The closest unification in spirit is the
structured state-space duality of Mamba-2 \cite{dao2024ssd}, which identifies selective SSMs with masked-attention variants through the
matrix algebra of semiseparable operators. Our unification runs along an orthogonal axis:
rather than relating SSMs \emph{to attention}, we exhibit the linear-SSM family \emph{itself}
as switch-restrictions of one affine prefix-scan operator with a nonlinear rapidity drive and
a bounded decoder, and read the family's shared behavior off its reproducing kernel ---
including the boundedness guarantee and the attractor topology, which the duality view does
not track. The HiPPO theory \cite{gu2020hippo} supplies the principled initialization of the
LTI corner of this family; the linear-attention and fast-weight lineage
\cite{katharopoulos2020linear,schlag2021fastweight} occupies the rank-$D$ rung of the
capacity ladder alongside DeltaNet.

Against attention \cite{vaswani2017attention} and the test-time-memory hybrids that pair
attention with a learned recurrent memory --- Titans \cite{behrouz2025titans} --- our result
explains \emph{why} such hybrids work: a single binding (attention) layer supplies the
outer-product rank that the bounded recurrent state structurally lacks, and the double
dissociation shows the recurrent scan class must be the interior-attractor kind for that binding
to land. The same reading covers Griffin \cite{de2024griffin}: a gated linear recurrence paired
with a local-attention layer is precisely the interior-attractor-plus-binding configuration the
dissociation predicts to succeed. The MQAR benchmark \cite{arora2024zoology} is exactly the instrument that exposes the
rank ceiling, and the holographic write \cite{plate1995holographic} is the in-state,
cache-free route up the ladder. We position GSSM not as a competitor within the family but as
its unification: the operator the others restrict, with a boundedness guarantee and a
length-invariance the encoded members give up by construction.

\section{Conclusion}
The geometric selective state-space model is the general input-controlled reproducing-kernel
operator of the linear-SSM family, verified to $\sim\!10^{-15}$, with its LTI restriction the
geometric Toeplitz kernel to $3.55\times10^{-15}$ and a $1.37\times10^{13}$ control separation.
One operator yields three consequences: the kernel carries no absolute position, so the model is
length-invariant by construction ($+243\%\to+2.6\%$ drift on removing the PE, a billion tokens at
constant memory); the per-channel readout is rank-1, which \emph{predicts} the associative-recall
wall the standard reading merely observes; and a double dissociation --- two layer-identical stacks
at $100\%$ vs.\ $16\%$ recall, differing only in scan class --- shows the kernel's attractor
topology causally gates whether attention can bind. The rigor is graded and labeled throughout ---
theorem, argument, analogy --- and every number is reproducible from a committed script and result
JSON.
